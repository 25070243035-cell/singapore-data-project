    How Sonar SG works

    1. Set your location

      Type an address or a 6-digit postal code, press for your device location, for coordinates, or just tap the map.

    2. Pick what you need

      Choose a service category, then optionally a service type such as Pharmacy or MRT Station Exit.

    3. Choose how to search

      Search within a radius, or ask for the nearest N places.

    4. Scan nearby

      Results come back sorted by distance. Click a card to fly to it, or click a marker to find its card.

    Within a radius

    Returns every matching place inside the circle, up to the maximum you set, nearest first. Good for “what's within walking distance?”.

    PostGIS: ST_DWithin(geog, point, radius) on a geography column, so distances are true metres on the WGS 84 spheroid.

    Nearest N

    Returns exactly the N closest places, however far they are. Good for rare services like hospitals or fire stations.

    PostGIS: ORDER BY ST_Distance(geog, point) LIMIT N. The ring stretches to the farthest result.

    If a radius search comes back empty, Sonar SG suggests the next radius up or switching to Nearest N.

      “All services” and the per-category layers are WMS images published by GeoServer from the same PostGIS table.

      Result pins use category colours and icons. Dense results group into clusters; click one to zoom in.

    • Heatmap

      Shows where the current results concentrate.

    • Scale & coordinates

      The scale bar and live cursor coordinates sit at the bottom-left of the map.
